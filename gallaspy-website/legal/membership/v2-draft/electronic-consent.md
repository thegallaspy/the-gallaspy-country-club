# THE GALLASPY GOLF CLUB
## Electronic Signature and Records Consent

DRAFT — SUBJECT TO LEGAL REVIEW

Members may affirmatively consent to electronic
agreements, signatures, billing disclosures and
membership-related records.

Typing a name and affirmatively submitting the required
consents constitutes the intended electronic signature.

Members must have access to readable copies of the
documents before signing and copies of accepted
agreements after enrollment.

Access requires a compatible browser or application,
internet access and the ability to retain electronic
documents.

Members may request paper copies or withdraw consent
to future electronic records through the Club's
official contact channel.

Withdrawal does not invalidate previously executed
agreements.

ELECTRONIC RECORDS AND MEMBER RIGHTS

Electronic consent applies to membership agreements,
billing disclosures, payment confirmations,
renewal notices and membership-related records.

Members may obtain paper copies without charge,
withdraw electronic consent without charge, or
update electronic contact information by contacting
info@thegallaspy.com.

Withdrawal does not itself cancel membership or
recurring billing, and does not invalidate previously
accepted agreements. Future required notices will
be provided through an appropriate alternative method.

Members must have access to a compatible browser
or application, internet access, a working email
address and the ability to save or print records.

Accepted membership documents will be retained
and made available to the member.
