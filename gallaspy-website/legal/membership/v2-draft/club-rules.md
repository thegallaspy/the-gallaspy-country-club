# THE GALLASPY GOLF CLUB
## Club Rules and Regulations

DRAFT — SUBJECT TO LEGAL REVIEW

Members shall demonstrate integrity, sportsmanship,
respect, responsible conduct and proper golf etiquette.

Members must comply with event rules, course policies,
safety instructions and lawful directions of club staff.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, violence,
cheating, fraud, unauthorized access and dangerous
conduct are prohibited.

Members shall not misuse club property, confidential
information, names, trademarks or membership credentials.

Membership privileges are personal and nontransferable.

Members are responsible for the conduct of their guests,
subject to applicable law and individual responsibility.

The Club may remove participants from an activity when
reasonably necessary for safety or orderly operations.

Permanent membership termination is subject to the
authority of the President and Vice President.

Falcon Society admission remains a separate,
approval-based membership process.

MEMBERSHIP ADMINISTRATION

Members must maintain accurate account information
and must not transfer or share membership credentials.

Questions, complaints and requests for review may
be submitted to info@thegallaspy.com.
