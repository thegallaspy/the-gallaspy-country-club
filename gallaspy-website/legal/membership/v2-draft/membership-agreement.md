# THE GALLASPY GOLF CLUB
## Standard Membership Agreement

DRAFT — SUBJECT TO LEGAL REVIEW

The Gallaspy Development Group, LLC, operating as The
Gallaspy Golf Club, administers Standard Membership.

1. MEMBERSHIP
Membership becomes effective after acceptance of the
required agreements and verification of all applicable
initial payments. Creating an app account does not
constitute club membership.

2. DUES
Monthly membership requires a $250 initiation fee and
$100 monthly dues. The initial total is $350.
Annual membership costs $1,000, with the initiation
fee waived. The selected plan renews automatically
unless canceled as provided in the billing disclosure.

3. PRIVILEGES
Standard Membership includes one organized Gallaspy
Round monthly, one additional golf activity monthly,
one networking or social opportunity monthly,
Golf Partners access, rewards, eligible apparel
discounts of up to 25%, and member priority access.

Priority registration is subject to availability,
eligibility and applicable event requirements.
GHIN services are subject to governing-body approval.
Green fees and separately priced activities are not
included unless expressly stated.

4. OWNERSHIP
Membership does not grant equity, ownership,
management authority, voting rights or property
interests in the company or future club facilities.
Membership proceeds support club operations and
future club development. Membership is not an investment.

5. CONDUCT
Members must comply with the Club Rules and
Regulations and applicable host facility policies.

6. CANCELLATION
Members may cancel recurring billing through their
membership account. Ordinary cancellation takes
effect at the end of the paid period. Fees for the
current period are not ordinarily refundable, except
where required by law or otherwise approved.

7. DISCIPLINE
Membership privileges may be suspended for safety,
misconduct, fraud or material policy violations.
Permanent termination requires authorization of
the President and Vice President, subject to law.

8. RIGHTS
Nothing in this agreement overrides applicable
consumer protection rights or other mandatory law.

9. MEMBERSHIP ADMINISTRATION

Standard Membership does not require a separate
application review. Activation requires acceptance
of the membership documents and verified payment
of all applicable initial charges.

Members may access their membership records through
My Gallaspy and request assistance through
info@thegallaspy.com.

Membership is separate from Falcon Society admission,
which remains subject to approval.
