# THE GALLASPY GOLF CLUB
## Electronic Signature and Records Consent

DRAFT — SUBJECT TO LEGAL REVIEW

Members may affirmatively consent to electronic
agreements, signatures, billing disclosures and
membership-related records.

Typing a name and affirmatively submitting the required
consents constitutes the intended electronic signature.

Members must have access to readable copies of the
documents before signing and copies of accepted
agreements after enrollment.

Access requires a compatible browser or application,
internet access and the ability to retain electronic
documents.

Members may request paper copies or withdraw consent
to future electronic records through the Club's
official contact channel.

Withdrawal does not invalidate previously executed
agreements.

The final contact procedure, paper-copy policy and
consequences of withdrawing consent must be established
before public enrollment.
